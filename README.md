# HR Employee Attrition Analysis

## Business Problem
A company is losing 16.1% of its workforce every year.
This dashboard identifies which departments, job roles and work patterns 
are driving employee attrition — helping HR teams take targeted action.

---

## Live Dashboard
[Click here to view the interactive dashboard](https://datastudio.google.com/reporting/6fae59f7-39c3-4e97-9f29-a08a79f4dde8)

---

## Key Business Insights

| Insight | Finding |
|---------|---------|
| Overall Attrition Rate | 16.1% — 237 out of 1,470 employees left |
| Highest Risk Department | Sales has highest attrition relative to size |
| Overtime Impact | Employees working overtime show 3x higher attrition risk |
| Highest Risk Job Role | Sales Executive and Laboratory Technician top attrition |
| Average Monthly Income | $6,502 — lower income linked to higher attrition |

---

## Business Recommendations

1. **Reduce overtime** — overtime workers are leaving at significantly higher rates; workload distribution needs review
2. **Focus retention on Sales team** — highest attrition department, likely due to high pressure and targets
3. **Review compensation for Lab Technicians and Sales Executives** — high volume roles with high attrition suggest pay dissatisfaction
4. **Early intervention program** — target employees with low income, high overtime and short tenure
